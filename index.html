Struggling with Execution?

As a Civil Engineer, I learned the complex, capital-intensive realities of the Utilities sector firsthand. But graduating into the unpredictability of 2020 demanded a pivot. I adapted by transitioning into marketing and Sales Operations within the automotive industry, shifting my focus from physical infrastructure to business strategy. Today, my STEM MBA serves as the bridge between those two worlds. I specialize in taking raw, "bottom-up" technical data and translating it into high-level, C-suite strategies that drive measurable margin growth.

It is about creative autonomy and systemic understanding. Before pivoting into the automotive industry, I operated at the intersection of design and digital commerce. Building digital assets for franchise businesses and launching Shopify ecosystems for entrepreneurs. Assisting with targeted ads, SEO Google Analytics, and customer journey mapping.

Coding this portfolio from first principles was a fun project alongside my MBA. I built the design freehand to match my strategic brand while demonstrating a fundamental truth: to showcase my understanding of technical architecture behind marketing analytics and simulation models. In a world of templates, I choose to build the sustained system.

I drove the shift toward a Just-In-Time (JIT) delivery model. In the Canadian automotive market, where seasonal demand is highly volatile and winter weather constantly threatens supply lines, stripping away safety stock is a massive commercial risk. To mitigate this, I restructured our vendor development strategy. By collaborating directly with upstream supplier R&D teams and running rigorous cost models, we executed the shift successfully. The result was a 10–15% reduction in stockouts, improving shelf availability during our peak seasons.
