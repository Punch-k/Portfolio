01. Pipeline by Stage

What is open, what is real, what is stuck

Each open opportunity is weighted by a stage probability. Deals past their close date count as slipped. Deals older than the stale limit are flagged for a clean up.

05. Territory and Quota Model

Fair books, honest quotas

Quota for each rep is book ARR times the growth target. Coverage compares weighted pipeline to quota. Use rebalance to move accounts between territories until books are even, then compare before and after.

Quota vs weighted pipeline by repDashed line is quota times the coverage you need.

Data and Method

About the data

  1. All 300 accounts, their opportunities and usage figures are synthetic. They were generated by a script with a fixed seed, so the demo is the same on every load. Account names are made up from word lists. Any match with a real company is a coincidence.
  2. Reps are labeled Rep A to Rep H. Territories are broad regions.
  3. Stage probabilities, health weights, churn rates by band, the growth target and the coverage ratio are assumptions you can change on the page.
  4. Everything on this page is calculated in your browser from data.json. There is no backend and no CRM connection.

This is a portfolio case study, not work done for an employer. It shows how I would structure CRM reporting and RevOps planning. It does not use or claim any live Salesforce, HubSpot or Dynamics 365 connection.
