01. Demand Review

Naive vs Seasonal vs ETS

Each method is trained on the months before the holdout window. Then it forecasts the holdout. Errors are measured against what the Census actually reported.

How the three methods work
Naive repeats the last known month. Seasonal naive repeats the same month from last year. ETS is Holt-Winters exponential smoothing with a damped trend. Its smoothing weights are fitted on the training months by grid search, unless you set them by hand below.
MAPE is the average percent miss per month. WAPE is total miss divided by total sales. Bias is total forecast minus total actual, as a percent. Positive bias means the plan ran high.

02. Safety Stock

Buffer by service level, lead time and variability

Safety stock covers the demand you did not forecast. Better forecasts mean smaller errors, and smaller errors mean less buffer for the same service.

APolicy inputsThese are planning choices, not data.
BDemand variability sourceBoth come from the data.
Formula
Safety stock = z × √( L × σ² + D² × σL² ). Here z comes from the service level, L is lead time plus review period in months, σ is the monthly demand uncertainty, D is average monthly demand, and σL is lead time variability in months.
